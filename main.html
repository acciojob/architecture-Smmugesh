Modern Architecture

Architecture is both the process and the product of planning, designing, and constructing buildings and other physical structures. Modern architecture focuses on simplicity, functionality, and innovation. It often uses glass, steel, and concrete materials to create elegant and sustainable structures.
Architectural styles have evolved throughout history, reflecting cultural, technological, and social changes. From ancient temples to futuristic skyscrapers, architecture shapes the environment where people live and work. Sustainable architecture is now becoming increasingly important worldwide.
